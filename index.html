Questions, answered

Everything people ask before their first hunt.

Short, straight answers — the same ones we'd give you on the phone.

What is Cletus Treasure Hunt?

It's a deal-hunting service. Twice a day it scans Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist, eBay and auction houses across the U.S., filters out junk and sold listings, and emails you a short list of live, on-target deals with direct links. You tell it what to find; it does the searching.

How much does a deal-hunting service cost?

It starts with a free first find. Paid plans are a one-time Single Hunt at $49, Standard at $99/month for up to 3 targets, Pro at $199/month with auction coverage and bid math, and Local Business at $299/month for equipment sourcing.

Does it work nationwide or only locally?

It works anywhere in the United States. You set the search radius — local pickup only, your whole state, or nationwide with shipping. The service adjusts which listings it surfaces to match the distance you're willing to travel or ship.

Which marketplaces does it search?

It watches 12+ sources nationwide including Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist, and eBay, plus national auction networks like GovDeals, Public Surplus, Municibid, GSA Auctions, AllSurplus, Proxibid and GovPlanet — and aggregators that span 40+ government auction sites at once. It checks places most people never look, twice a day, so fresh listings are caught within hours.

Will it buy or bid on items for me?

No. Cletus Treasure Hunt is read-only deal scouting. It finds and ranks live listings and sends you direct links, but it never contacts sellers, places bids, or spends money on your behalf. You stay in full control of every purchase.
